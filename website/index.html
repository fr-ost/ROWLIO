Everything you need

A complete extraction toolkit, not a table copier.

Tables, done properly

Merged cells, multi-row headers, nested tables, hidden rows, ARIA grids and tables that load later. Anything unusual becomes a visible warning, never silent data loss.

Visual selector

Point and click. Similar items and their fields are found for you: title, price, image, link, rating, date.

CSS & XPath

Write your own selectors with live match counts, samples and on-page highlighting.

Pagination & infinite scroll

Follow next links, page numbers or endless feeds, always within limits you set and with a Stop button.

Batch URLs

Run one extraction over a list of pages, such as the detail pages behind a listing.

Clean before you export

A fast spreadsheet-style preview: edit cells, sort, search, filter, remove duplicates, find & replace, split and combine columns, extract numbers, parse dates. Full undo, and the original extraction is always kept.

Links, images, contacts

Links with filters, images including lazy-loaded ones, emails, phone numbers and social profiles.

Metadata & structured data

Open Graph, Twitter Cards, JSON-LD and Schema.org microdata, flattened into columns.

Projects & recipes

Save results on your device and reuse extraction settings on similar pages.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no usage limits, no locked features, no trial and no account. {{name}} is a product of {{company}}, built to be useful to everyone.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Extraction, cleaning and export all happen inside your browser. Data is saved on your device only when you choose to save a project, and you can delete it at any time.

Does it work on every website?

It works on pages you can open in your browser. Chrome does not allow any extension on its own pages (chrome://), the Chrome Web Store and a few protected pages. Content inside cross-origin frames is out of reach.

Can it get around logins, paywalls or CAPTCHAs?

No, and it will not. {{name}} reads what your browser already shows you. Please respect each website's terms of use and applicable laws.

Does it handle non-English text?

Yes. Everything is Unicode end to end, tested with বাংলা, emoji and more, including the CSV and Excel exports.

Is it a crawler?

No. Pagination, infinite scroll and batch runs are started by you, bounded by limits you set, and can be stopped at any moment.
