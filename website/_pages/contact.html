Reporting a bug

A report that includes the following can usually be acted on straight away:

  • the address of the page, if it is public;
  • what you clicked, what you expected and what happened instead;
  • your {{name}} version (shown under Help & About in the extension) and your Chrome version;
  • a screenshot, if the problem is visible.

Please do not send passwords, private data or the contents of pages that are not yours to share.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security problem in {{name}}, please report it privately through the Telegram contact above instead of posting it publicly, and allow time for a fix before disclosing it.
