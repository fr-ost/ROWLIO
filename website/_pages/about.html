What {{name}} is

{{name}} is a Chrome extension for getting data out of web pages and into a spreadsheet. It reads tables, product listings, search results, links, images and page metadata from the page you have open, lets you review and clean the rows, and saves them as a file. {{tagline}}

It is free, with no account, no usage limit and no paid tier. Extraction, cleaning and export all happen inside your browser, so the data you collect is not sent anywhere.

Why it exists

Copying data from a web page by hand is slow and error-prone, and most tools that automate it ask for a subscription, an account, or for your data to pass through their servers. {{name}} was built to do the everyday cases well without any of those conditions: a table into Excel, a product list into a CSV, the links on a page into a sheet.

What we hold to

  • Local by design. The extension makes no network requests of its own. There is no server to send data to.
  • Minimal access. {{name}} can read a page only after you click its icon there.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising.
  • Respect for websites. {{name}} does not bypass logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs or anti-bot protections, and it is not a crawler. Multi-page runs are started by you, limited and stoppable.
  • Honest limits. What the extension cannot do is written down, on the FAQ and support pages.
